# Automation Governance Product Positioning Policy

Status: accepted

## Decision

Pancakes is an IR-centered automation governance and hardening engine with Make as its first
implemented language adapter. Its repository-visible product framing is deterministic validation,
repair guidance, audit-safe evidence projection, and client-safe handoff for automation blueprints.

The product is not positioned as a generic JSON linter, a certification tool, a legal-compliance
engine, a completed cross-platform IR, or an official platform extension. It treats automation
blueprints as analyzable program artifacts and keeps current claims tied to local static evidence.

Source review was refreshed on 2026-05-13 against official Make terms, Make API documentation,
Make Custom Apps review and community-app terms, Make brand guidance, Make website copyright
notice, and public software-interoperability source material. This review is an engineering risk
boundary and is not legal advice.

## Product-Safe Wording

- Production-readiness for automation workflows.
- Deterministic validation and repair guidance for generated scenarios.
- Governance for agent-authored automation.
- Client-safe operational handoff.
- Static analysis and safety checks for automation blueprints.
- Enterprise trust layer for AI-generated workflows.
- Make-compatible static analysis when the input is a Make-exported or Make-shaped automation
  artifact and the output is limited to local evidence.

## Forbidden Public Claims

- Do not claim legal compliance, certification, attestation readiness, or audit
completion.
- Do not claim affiliation, endorsement, sponsorship, partnership, certification,
  app-review approval, Marketplace listing, Community Apps approval, or official extension status
  from Make.com or any platform provider.
- Do not claim affiliation, endorsement, or certification by Make.com.
- Do not claim Zapier, n8n, Python translation, or cross-platform IR support
until those adapters and validation suites exist.
- Do not expose internal rule-corpus mechanics, candidate IDs, private source
ledgers, or implementation details in customer-facing output.
- Do not compare against visual automation platforms by insulting their product
category.
- Do not use Make logos, screenshots, brand assets, copied documentation bodies, or docs-derived
  catalogs in public output without a recorded rights basis.
- Do not present Pancakes as a substitute for Make support, Make app review, Make API checkups,
  customer legal review, customer security review, or platform-provider approval.

## Make Platform Framing Boundary

Make.com is a named source platform and the first implemented language adapter. It is not the
Pancakes product model. Pancakes may say it analyzes Make-shaped automation artifacts when local
fixtures, parsers, validators, and report scrubbers support that claim. Pancakes must say future
Zapier, n8n, Python-output, or other platform work is deferred until there is a separate adapter,
fixture corpus, translation contract, and validation suite.

Make API and custom-app material may support interoperability research, authenticated operator
tasks, and source citation. It must not be used to imply a Make partnership, app listing,
third-party-service warranty, or approval to redistribute Make documentation, raw specifications,
customer payloads, or brand assets.

Public and customer-facing wording must stay close to local static evidence. Use "static analysis",
"repair guidance", "handoff evidence", "Make-compatible artifact validation", or "language adapter"
instead of "certified by Make", "official Make platform", "approved integration", "compliant",
"audit ready", or "guaranteed production safe".

## Required Evidence Before Stronger Claims

Stronger repository-visible positioning requires a reliable Make-adapter demo path, client-safe
report output, deterministic validation coverage, repair or handoff evidence, and measured product
outcomes such as prevented failures, reduced review time, or safer release decisions.

Sensitive commercial planning, personal planning, and private market hypotheses belong outside this
repository.
